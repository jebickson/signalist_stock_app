## Signalist – Stock Market Intelligence Platform

**Track the market. Analyze stocks. Make informed decisions.**

Signalist is a full-stack stock market tracking and intelligence platform designed to simplify financial market monitoring through real-time market data, interactive dashboards, personalized watchlists, and AI-powered insights.

### Explore Signalist

#### 📊 Stock Market Dashboard

Monitor stock prices, market movements, and financial information through an interactive dashboard.

#### 🔍 Stock Discovery

Search and explore stocks using company names and stock symbols.

#### ⭐ Personalized Watchlist

Add your favourite stocks and monitor their performance in one place.

#### 🔔 Price Alerts

Configure stock price alerts and receive notifications when your selected conditions are met.

#### 🤖 AI-Powered Insights

Generate AI-powered market summaries to understand financial information more easily.

#### ⚙️ Automated Workflows

Use background workflows to automate scheduled tasks and notifications.

### Technology Stack

Next.js · React · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · MongoDB · Finnhub API · Google Gemini · Inngest
